# Release: Resolver v3.1.1 - Advanced Control & A Cleaner Config

Config gets a Mode selector. Resolver now opens Config in a clean **Simple** mode with only the essentials, and every other option moves behind **Advanced Control**, switched on by holding a CPU until it powers up the app. The search bar shrinks to an icon, notice links open in a proper browser tab, and toasts are readable in light mode.

This update modifies Web UI client assets only, and installs over the air on Resolver v3.1. No new APK is needed.

---

## Change Log

### 1. Mode Selector & Advanced Control
- **Simple Mode:** Config now shows only what you need to connect and manage the app: **Connection** (all of it), **Dock style**, **Light mode**, **Visible tabs** and **System Settings**. Everyone starts in Simple, including after this update.
- **Advanced Control:** Shows every option in Config, exactly as before: Prompts, Magic Prompt, Generation, Live Preview, History & Results, Inpaint & Analyze, Models, Extensions, Diagnostics, Experimental and the rest of Interface.
- **Hold to Activate:** Tap the new **Mode selector** bar at the top of Config, then hold the CPU until its bar fills. Energy runs from the CPU to the app, and the Advanced Control sections fade in. Letting go early cancels it.
- **Theme Colours:** The CPU, its wiring and the energy take the current tab's accent (SDXL, Flux, Qwen, Anima, Krea), in dark and light mode.
- **Remembered:** Your mode stays as you left it after closing the app. **SWITCH TO SIMPLE** in the same window goes back with one tap.
- **Nothing Turned Off:** Options hidden in Simple keep working exactly as they are set.

### 2. Config Search
- **Search Icon:** The big search bar is now a small icon beside the Mode selector. Tap it to search, and the X to close the search.
- **Searches Your Mode:** In Simple, search finds only the options Simple shows; in Advanced Control, every option.

### 3. Fixes
- **Notice Links:** A link in an in-app notice now opens in a browser tab with a close button, instead of loading inside Resolver with no way back.
- **Toasts in Light Mode:** Short messages at the bottom of the screen were dark text on a dark background in light mode. They now have a light background.

---

## Installation

On **Resolver v3.1**: open the app and tap **INSTALL UPDATE** when asked (or **Config → System Settings → UPDATE CHECK**). On an older version: install the **v3.1 APK** from the releases page first. Your images and settings stay.
