# Release: Resolver v3.0 - Wildcards, Forge Couple & The Biggest Update Yet

This release is the largest update in Resolver's history. It introduces a full wildcard system, Forge Couple regional prompting, prompt history and tag autocomplete, advanced LoRA management, Backup & Restore, a redesigned interface with a new sliding dock, and more than 40 bug fixes across the whole app.

This update modifies Web UI client assets and native-level code, requiring a manual installation build.

---

## Credit & Shoutout

A huge shoutout to contributor **Bungles (Oliver Lufti)** for the entire **v3.0 patch**, delivered in two parts:
- **Part 1 (Essential Fixes):** More than 40 fixes to things v2.9 already had: JPEG/WEBP metadata support, LoRA and style tag handling, Qwen Hi-Res values, preset ordering, launch flashes, scrolling, the image viewer, Crop & Edit and notifications.
- **Part 2 (Extras):** The wildcard system, Forge Couple, prompt history, tag autocomplete, Magic Prompt DESCRIBE, LoRA variants and groups, Backup & Restore, gallery favourites and compare, the redesigned Queue tab and one consistent look across the whole app.

## Change Log

### 1. Wildcards System
- **`__name__` Wildcards:** Wildcards become a random option when the job runs, with weights, nesting, Dynamic Prompts syntax, labels, conditions and rules.
- **Wildcards Window:** Search, favourites, tap to send to a prompt box, and an editor with BASIC and ADVANCED views.
- **Import Anywhere:** Import from files, a URL, a Civitai link or saved library links ("Bungles library" included by default). Same-name wildcards can be replaced, merged or kept, and saved links can be checked for updates. Nothing is downloaded until you choose to.
- **Reroll:** From the image viewer, keep some of an image's wildcard picks and redraw the others.
- **Versions & Undo:** A wildcard's last 10 saves, UNDO after a delete, and Recently Deleted (30 days).
- **Export:** Pick wildcards, favourites or your own, and save or share them as JSON.

### 2. Prompting Power Tools
- **Prompt History:** Each tab's last 20 prompts and negatives, restored with their styles, LoRAs and sliders.
- **Color-Coded Prompts:** Bracket levels, unmatched brackets in red, weights, LoRAs and wildcards highlighted in prompt boxes, Analyze and Queue details.
- **Tag Autocomplete:** Through the TagComplete Neo extension (off by default).
- **`style::`, `random::` and `order::`:** Use a style for one image, or pick a style, character, LoRA or image size per image at random or in turn.
- **All Combinations:** Generate every combination of a prompt's choices and wildcards, up to a limit you set.
- **Prompt Guide:** A `?` beside each tab's prompt buttons explains weights, BREAK, AND, LoRAs, wildcards and more, with colored examples.
- **Magic Prompt Upgrades:** A new DESCRIBE tab describes an image as tags or sentences with a vision model, and Scene Optimization picks wildcard options that suit each other.

### 3. Forge Couple & Generation
- **Forge Couple (SDXL, Anima):** Region boxes under the prompt, with BASIC (direction with a preview) and ADVANCED (draw each region) modes. Couple layouts can be saved as styles.
- **Format Button:** Tap to cycle PNG, JPEG and WEBP, with a JPEG/WEBP quality box in Config.
- **Clear VRAM:** A bin beside GENERATE replaces UNLOAD MODELS and confirms with the server's VRAM use.
- **SKIP:** Skip the current image while the rest of a batch continues.
- **More Settings:** Hi-Res sampler, schedule, checkpoint and prompts; variation seed, refiner, tiling and Clip Skip.
- **Outpainting:** A Crop & Edit crop beyond the image fills and masks the new area, then switches to Inpaint.
- **Redesigned Queue Tab:** Compact cards with settings chips, a progress line on the running job and thumbnails on finished ones.

### 4. LoRA & Style Management
- **LoRA Variants:** Alternative versions of a LoRA (an outfit, say), each with its own name, weight, prompts and thumbnail.
- **LoRA Groups:** LoRAs under one header, one in the prompt at a time, with a shared Type.
- **Weight Sliders:** Under the prompt box while a LoRA's tag is there, following the tag both ways.
- **Custom Thumbnails & Content Search:** Choose thumbnails, and search prompts, negatives, variants and file names.
- **Styles Overhaul:** Styles now look like the LoRA list, with thumbnails, favourites, partial-apply highlighting, and styles on img2img.
- **Sorting:** A–Z, Recently Added, Recently Used and Most Used in Wildcards, Styles, LoRA and SAAC.

### 5. History, Gallery & Viewer
- **Faster History:** History reads a light copy of each image (150 large PNGs: 470 MB and 2s down to 60 MB and 0.2s).
- **Search, Favourites & Date Sections:** Search by prompt, LoRA, checkpoint or date. Favourites are kept from the Gallery Cache Limit and DELETE ALL.
- **Compare & Share:** Compare in the image viewer, hold a result to see its source, and share images outside the app.
- **Multi-Select:** Long-press to enter select mode, drag across images to select many, and share, favourite or delete them together.
- **Gallery Badges & Columns:** Format, HR, INP, ADetailer, ControlNet, Forge Couple and Upscale badges, plus AUTO/3/4/5 columns.

### 6. Interface & Navigation
- **New Sliding Dock:** A pill now glides between tabs. Tap a tab, or slide your finger along the dock to highlight one (its name appears above it) and release to switch. Haptics tick as you pass each tab and confirm on release. Sliding up off the dock cancels.
- **One Consistent Look:** Shared font sizes, spacing, windows, buttons and colors, with every dropdown opening as a flyout.
- **Reorganized Config:** 13 sections, one open at a time, with Config Search. Some options have been renamed or moved.
- **Dock Style & Notification Icon:** Choose a dock style, and choose the notification icon with ADetailer progress shown in it.
- **Show UI Hints:** Hide the grey how-to text across the app.

### 7. Backup & Restore
- **Full Backup & Restore:** Back up and restore Settings, Presets, Styles, LoRA Data, Wildcards, Comfy Templates and, optionally, your Civitai API Key and History Favourites.

### 8. Bug Fixes & Performance
- **JPEG and WEBP Results Work Everywhere:** Analyze, seeds, Copy Metadata, prompt import and Keep Source Seed now read their metadata.
- **No More Black Frames at Launch:** The splash screen stays until the app has drawn, and settings no longer flash into place.
- **Faster Redraws:** Icons are drawn once, so a LoRA list redraw went from 1.1s to 0.2s.
- **Generation Fixes:** Qwen Hi-Res sends Denoise 0 and Steps 0 correctly, and img2img presets no longer reset steps, CFG, sampler and schedule.
- **LoRA & Style Fixes:** Select LoRA no longer spins forever, removing a LoRA or style removes all of its tags, and `styles.csv` handles quotes, commas and line breaks.
- **Touch & Scroll Fixes:** No accidental tab changes from swipes, tabs keep their scroll position, and the image viewer, Crop & Edit pinch zoom and sliders behave correctly.
- **Persistent Settings:** VRAM Profile is now saved, and lowering the Gallery Cache Limit no longer deletes images while you type.
- **Notification Icons:** Notifications no longer show a white square.

## Update Instructions

> [!NOTE]
> **Manual Installation Required**: This release includes native changes and requires manual installation. Install the updated APK directly over your existing application.

1. **Download APK:** Grab `Resolverv3.0.apk` from the downloads section above.
2. **Install:** Install the APK manually over your existing Resolver installation. Do **not** uninstall first. All local configurations, history and gallery images will remain intact.
3. **First Launch:** If you had installed an earlier OTA update, v3.0 automatically switches to its own built-in files on first launch. No action needed.

> [!TIP]
> Some new features use optional Forge extensions: **Forge Couple** (`sd-forge-couple`) for regional prompting and **TagComplete Neo** (`sd-webui-tagcomplete-neo`) for tag autocomplete. Everything else works without them.
